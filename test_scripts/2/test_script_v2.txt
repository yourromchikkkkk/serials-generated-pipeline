Odysseus stands at the edge of the battlefield and watches the distant enemy fires. Achilles approaches, tightening the strap on his armor.

Odysseus: "A wise man wins with his mind."

Achilles: "A remembered man wins with his courage."

Odysseus looks at him in silence before replying, "The dead remember nothing."

Achilles smiles faintly, turns toward the battlefield, and says, "Then I'll leave the remembering to the living."
